# Predictive Supply Planning Model & Customer Retention Dashboard

### Supply Chain Analytics | Washington University in St. Louis – Olin Business School

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was developed as part of my **Supply Chain Analytics academic work at Washington University in St. Louis – Olin Business School**.

The project combines **predictive analytics, supply-chain planning, customer analytics, and interactive dashboard development** to transform historical data into actionable business insights.

The analysis focuses on two business use cases:

1. **Predictive Supply Planning**
2. **Customer Retention & Churn Analysis**

---

## 🎯 Objectives

### Predictive Supply Planning

* Analyze historical supply and demand patterns
* Identify trends and variability
* Develop predictive models to support future planning
* Evaluate model performance
* Generate insights for inventory and supply planning
* Support data-driven operational decisions

### Customer Retention Dashboard

* Analyze customer purchasing behavior
* Identify customer segments
* Measure retention and churn
* Identify high-value and at-risk customers
* Monitor key customer KPIs
* Translate customer analytics into actionable retention strategies
